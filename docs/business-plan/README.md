## Information to collect

Nexus is a desktop personal agent with website capability generation and capability execution. The financial model needs inputs for both activities; this checklist supplies no pricing or forecasts.

| Area | Inputs needed |
| --- | --- |
| Customer and pricing | Target users, value delivered, charging unit (subscription, run, or capability), proposed plans/limits, and who pays model/runtime costs |
| Demand and revenue | Expected paying users, conversion, retention/churn, runs per user, capability-generation frequency, and billing period; label assumptions |
| LLM costs | Provider/model, dated rates, measured input/output/cached tokens per successful task, and extra usage from failures/retries |
| Runtime costs | Agent37 billing basis and measured runtime, browser execution duration, local-vs-hosted compute, and any future publisher costs actually incurred |
| Other costs | neatlogs subscription/limits, support effort, distribution, payment fees, and fixed operating expenses; separate recurring costs from hackathon credits |
| Unit economics | Revenue and variable cost per user/run/capability, gross margin, retry overhead, and break-even volume using documented assumptions |
| Cash and runway | Actual starting cash, funding/credit balances and expiry, monthly cash inflows/outflows, one-time costs, and runway under each scenario |
| Scenarios | Base/downside/upside inputs and sensitivity to model usage, failure rate, pricing, and adoption; no invented measurements |

For each input record its unit, date, source, whether it is measured or assumed, and whether the expense is paid by Nexus or by the user. Collect real traces and provider invoices/usage reports when available. Check current vendor prices before entering rates; no rates are asserted here.
