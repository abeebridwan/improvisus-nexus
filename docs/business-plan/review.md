As of October 12, 2026 (WAT): original cfo.ai links and public metadata verified; plan contents unavailable to this session. The builder reports cfo.ai generated the plans. No financial conclusion below is presented as a verified cfo.ai result.

## Business positioning

Improvisus is building the capability layer for the agentic web. Nexus is the proposed Electron desktop personal agent: an individual makes one website workflow agent-ready through generated WebMCP, then discovers and executes existing WebMCP capabilities across websites. Individual users and developers are the proposed customer groups. The current repository is a desktop foundation; product execution is not yet implemented.

## Financial risks and reviewer recommendations

These are provisional review questions for this business model, **not findings from the unread cfo.ai plan**. They introduce no financial results or approved implementation changes.

| Potential risk | Recommendation for review |
| --- | --- |
| Inference, retries or browser costs exceed the model | Compare assumed cost per successful task with real neatlogs/provider measurements once execution exists; validate limits and pricing against those measurements. |
| Free usage or heavy developer usage creates a subsidy | Check that the model includes free-user costs, expected utilization and a high-usage sensitivity. |
| Forecast acquisition/conversion is mistaken for traction | Label customer/revenue projections as assumptions and compare them with pilot retention and willingness-to-pay evidence. |
| Hosted harness/tool fees or support labour are omitted | Confirm all company-paid costs and avoid double-counting inference already billed by a runtime vendor. |
| Credits, prizes or unpaid founder work hide recurring burn | Separate recurring economics from temporary credits and disclose compensation assumptions. |
| Currency, taxes, refunds or payment collection weaken cash | Confirm billing jurisdiction/currency, fee treatment and cash timing before making a runway claim. |
| Premium-model routing or website changes alter unit costs | Check a downside sensitivity for larger token budgets and more retesting; preserve cfo.ai's original scenario outputs. |
